# Website updates

## What an admin can update

An admin can manage families and members, parish events, Bethkati issues, Mass timings, catechism timings, priests, gallery albums and contact enquiries. Online donations can also be turned on or off from the settings page.

Photographers only have access to the gallery section. They can create an album and upload photographs, but they cannot open the other admin pages.

## Gallery

Each event can have its own album. People can open the shared album link and download only the photographs they want. This is easier than sending many full-size photographs to a WhatsApp group at the same time.

The album can be shared directly through WhatsApp or another app on a phone. Visitors can also like individual photographs after signing in.

## Online donations

Work on Razorpay payments was started. Razorpay asked for more parish documents, including a power of attorney, so the online donation process had to be paused for now.
